# REFERENCE BASIS

The two packs supplied by the client drive `model/schemes.mjs`, sheet **X-101**
and `docs/07-seven-schemes.md`.

## What is here
- The Gauntlet system documents, verbatim, as the method being followed.
- The source manifests: every Rural Studio / Front Porch and Olson Kundig
  reference by **official URL**, with the published Front Porch areas,
  footprints, porch areas and perimeters as CSV.

## What is deliberately NOT here
The reference photographs and plan PDFs themselves. They are the property of
Rural Studio / Auburn University and Olson Kundig, they are hosted by their
publishers, and this container has no outbound access to fetch them anyway.
The manifests point at the originals; the packs' HTML walls load them when
opened on a machine with internet.

## What was extracted
Only the measurable, transferable facts — areas, footprints, perimeters, and
the design operation each precedent demonstrates. Every scheme in
`model/schemes.mjs` records which reference it came from, what operation it
borrowed, and what it must NOT copy, because the operation travels and the
form does not.
