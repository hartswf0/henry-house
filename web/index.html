NEXT BIGGEST IMPROVEMENT

The site and grading plan (C-101).

Three sheets now describe a building; none describes the ground. Everything the brief calls hardest — access, driveway grade, cut and fill balance, the septic field, the water source, the erosion control line, where stormwater actually goes — lives on a drawing that does not yet exist. Until it does, the house is a well-drawn object floating on an assumed plane.

After that, in order: systems routing (water, waste, air, power drawn as real networks through the spine, with a clash check that proves pipes do not pass through beams), then elevations and the 3D model, then envelope details carrying the pen test.
